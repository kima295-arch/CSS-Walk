My Trader Joe's Run

1:35PM on Thursday

Today, I decided to take a little trip to Trader Joe's. I grabbed my blue bag and walked 6 minutes downwards.

When I arrived, there were less people than usual, not even a checkout line. Even though I'm dorming, I still keep some important things in stock.

My Grocery List

When I went to the vegetables section, there was a lady that refused to move out of the way for me to reach the carrots on the top shelf.

I waited there for 10 minutes while she stared at nothing until a man pushed her to move. I was so thankful since I was too scared myself to speak up.

After walking around the store for a little longer, I finally made my way to the checkout which had a longer line than before. I paid for everything, packed my fruits into my bag, and headed back home.
